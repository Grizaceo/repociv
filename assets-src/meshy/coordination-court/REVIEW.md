## Actual image inspection

A broad low U-shaped ensemble has a large rear meeting hall, two shorter forward wings and an open central stone courtyard. A circular raised stone table sits at its center. Pale walls and stepped plinths, dark slate hipped roofs, brown/copper-colored framing and a small amber light above the rear arched entrance provide family continuity. The entrance stair and courtyard distinguish it from the vertical Archive and Observatory. The camera is elevated but nearly frontal and symmetric, rather than a strong three-quarter isometric view.

Material caveat: brown framing and shutters read more as timber than metallic aged copper; a concept image cannot establish physical material properties. It resembles a civic council house, with no literal software coordination symbols. This is intended metaphor, not proof the function will read at small map scale.

Reconstruction risks: narrow roof-wall junctions, the shaded corridor sides, the table underside and legs, front low balustrades and the central stair/door arch. The front has low walls with slim horizontal openings despite the request to avoid railings; check whether these remain robust in a reconstructed mesh. Window panels appear closed in this image, not the ragged perforations seen in Workshop geometry. Covered wings look intact, while the courtyard is intentionally open.
