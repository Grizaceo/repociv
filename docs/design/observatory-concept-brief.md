## Design

A compact stepped pale-stone podium supports a slender observation tower. Broad dark-slate eaves, aged copper trim and one substantial fixed lens/instrument crown, with a restrained turquoise accent. The crown must not copy the Archive lantern. Keep architectural surfaces closed and simple; window panels are shallow framed recesses, not perforated latticework. Avoid exposed gear teeth, railings, thin struts, tiny disconnected instruments and fragile mesh details. More vertical than the Workshop, more instrument-like and slender than the Archive. Same civilization, distinct function.

Tool: Meshy Image-to-Image. Model proposed: nano-banana-pro. One concept, advertised 9 credits; confirm before POST. Reference: `assets-src/meshy/memory-archive/textured/archive-textured.png`. Prompt:

Create ONE original Observatory building for the same stylized strategy-game civilization as the reference Memory Archive. Use the reference as architectural and material art direction, not the building to copy. A compact polygonal stepped warm pale limestone podium supports a slender observation tower, with broad dark slate eaves and aged copper trim. Crown it with one substantial fixed optical instrument or lens housing integrated into the masonry, with a subtle turquoise glass accent. The silhouette must read as observation and telemetry, not a temple, library lantern, modern radar dish or lighthouse. Taller and more slender than the Archive, with a few broad clear terraces, not a stack of tiny floors. Use solid closed surfaces, simple shallow rectangular window panels and thick reliable architectural members; no punched-out windows, latticework, thin railings, cables or floating pieces. Readable from elevated isometric strategy-game cameras. Show the complete standalone building in one clean elevated three-quarter view, plain neutral background, no ground tile or scenery, no people, text or logos, no dramatic baked lighting or excessive grime.

## Review gate

Concept approval first; actual geometry and cost are a later decision. Inspect the instrument for thick connected forms that can survive reconstruction. Do not spend more geometry/texture credits automatically or restart the deferred Workshop repair.
