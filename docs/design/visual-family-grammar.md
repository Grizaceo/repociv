## Shared grammar

- Broad terraces, stepped polygonal volumes, substantial eaves and deliberate architectural hierarchy.
- Warm pale stone, dark slate/ceramic roofs, aged copper structural accents; small amber activity accents. Turquoise reserved for observation/connections.
- A compact footprint, a grounded solid base, and negative space readable from elevated strategy cameras.
- Large material regions, restrained wear, no tiny surface greebles, labels, logos or realistic photographic grime.
- Roofs must not all become lantern towers; distinguish each function first by massing and silhouette.

## Execution Workshop brief

A single compact static building. Two broad stepped wings around a recessed work entrance, with a low rear service stack; width dominates height. A strong silhouette as an artisan/compute workshop, not a modern factory, house, temple or library. No machinery floating outside, no people, text, cogwheel insignias or thin railings. The archive reference guides architectural language and palette, not object identity.

Route: one image-to-image concept using an actual local Archive reference; approval checkpoint; then one image-to-3D untextured Smart Topology candidate, Meshy T2.5, target 4000 triangles, GLB only. Review geometry next to Archive before paid texturing. No blind paid retries, no variants, no automatic refine/remesh.
